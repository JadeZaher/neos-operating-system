# voluntary-exit

## C. Trigger Conditions

- **Member declaration**: any ecosystem member declares their intent to depart, verbally or in writing, to any steward or governance facilitator
- **Inactivity threshold**: a member has not participated in any governance activity for 90 consecutive days and does not respond to a 14-day reachability check
- **Agreement expiry without renewal**: a member's foundational agreement (e.g., UAF) expires and the member does not renew within the grace period
- **Ecosystem transition**: an ETHOS dissolves (per ethos-dissolution) and a member chooses not to transfer to another ETHOS

## D. Required Inputs

- **Departing member identity**: confirmed ecosystem identity of the person departing
- **Departure scope**: whether the member is leaving a single ETHOS or the entire ecosystem
- **Commitment inventory**: a complete list of the member's current roles, agreements, resource allocations, pending proposals, and active conflict resolutions -- generated by cross-referencing the agreement-registry, role-assignment records, and decision logs
- **Departure timeline**: the member's preferred departure date (minimum 7 days for urgent exits, default 30-day handoff period)
- **Data export preferences**: the member's choices about which governance participation records to include in their portable record (per portable-record skill)

## E. Step-by-Step Process

1. **Receive departure declaration.** The departing member notifies any steward or governance facilitator. No approval, justification, or waiting period is required to initiate exit. The facilitator acknowledges receipt within 48 hours and assigns a departure coordinator from outside the member's primary circle to prevent social pressure to stay.
2. **Generate commitment inventory.** The departure coordinator compiles the member's full commitment inventory by querying the agreement-registry, role-assignment records, resource allocation ledger, proposal registry, and conflict resolution logs. The inventory is shared with the departing member for verification within 5 days.
3. **Establish departure timeline.** The departing member and coordinator agree on a handoff timeline. The default is 30 days. The member may request a 7-day urgent timeline if personal circumstances require it. The ecosystem cannot extend the timeline beyond 30 days or use the handoff period as a retention mechanism.
4. **Initiate commitment unwinding.** The departure coordinator triggers the commitment-unwinding skill, which processes each commitment category: role obligations, agreement obligations, economic commitments, pending proposals, and active conflict resolutions. Each commitment is resolved with a receiving party acknowledgment.
5. **Execute data export.** The departing member exercises their right to a portable governance record (per the portable-record skill). The member selects which details to include beyond the mandatory minimum (identity and departure status). The export is generated and delivered to the member before the departure date.
6. **Confirm handoff completion.** The departure coordinator verifies that all commitments have been resolved or transferred. Any commitments that remain unresolved enter the emergency-transfer queue for the ETHOS to handle post-departure. The departing member is not held responsible for commitments the ecosystem fails to reassign.
7. **Record formal departure.** The departure coordinator creates a Departure Record using `assets/departure-record-template.yaml`, documenting the departure date, scope, commitment resolution status, and re-entry eligibility. The record is filed in the ecosystem's governance memory (Layer IX) and a copy is provided to the departing member.
8. **Communicate departure.** The ecosystem publishes a factual departure notice to affected circles and the broader membership. The notice contains only the member's name, departure date, and any roles that need filling. No editorial commentary, no reasons unless the departing member explicitly requests inclusion. Departure is communicated as a neutral governance event.

## F. Output Artifact

A Departure Record following `assets/departure-record-template.yaml`. The record contains: departure ID, member identity, departure scope (ETHOS or ecosystem), declaration date, effective departure date, commitment unwinding summary (referencing the Commitment Unwinding Ledger), data export confirmation, re-entry eligibility status, and coordinator identity. The record is accessible to the departing member indefinitely and to ecosystem governance records for historical reference.

## G. Authority Boundary Check

- **Any member** can initiate their own departure at any time without approval from any body
- **No individual, circle, or council** (including OSC) can block, delay, or condition a departure on fulfillment of obligations beyond the 30-day handoff maximum
- **The departure coordinator** facilitates the process but cannot require the member to complete tasks beyond the agreed timeline
- **If a member holds a sole critical role**, the ecosystem activates graceful degradation protocols (emergency role coverage, not exit prevention)
- **The departing member** retains full control over their data export choices and re-entry eligibility status
- **OSC** is notified of departures but has no gate authority over the process

## H. Capture Resistance Check

**Retention capture.** The 30-day maximum handoff period prevents ecosystems from using indefinite obligation lists to trap members. The 7-day urgent exit option ensures that even members in distress can leave quickly. No commitment can extend the departure timeline beyond 30 days -- any unresolved commitment transfers to the ecosystem's emergency queue.

**Social pressure capture.** The departure coordinator is drawn from outside the member's primary circle, preventing intimate relationships from being weaponized as retention tools. Departure notices are factual and neutral -- no narrative framing that implies betrayal or failure.

**Information capture.** The portable-record skill guarantees data export rights. The ecosystem cannot withhold a member's governance participation history as leverage to prevent departure. The member's data is theirs.

**Critical-role capture.** A sole critical role holder cannot be prevented from leaving. The ecosystem must maintain graceful degradation capacity -- if a single departure can collapse governance, the structural fragility is the ecosystem's problem, not the departing member's obligation.

## I. Failure Containment Logic

- **Coordinator unavailable**: if no coordinator can be assigned within 48 hours, the departing member may self-coordinate using the departure checklist (`assets/departure-checklist.yaml`) and any steward may sign off on the Departure Record
- **Commitment inventory incomplete**: if records are insufficient to generate a complete inventory, the coordinator documents known commitments and the member departs with a noted gap -- the ecosystem cannot delay departure for record-keeping failures
- **Receiving party refuses handoff**: if no one accepts a transferred commitment, the commitment enters the emergency-transfer queue and the ecosystem's governance facilitator is responsible for resolution post-departure
- **Member becomes unreachable during handoff**: if the departing member stops responding during the handoff period, the coordinator completes the process unilaterally on the declared departure date, filing a Departure Record with a "member unreachable" notation
- **Sole critical role with no successor**: the ETHOS activates emergency role coverage (Layer VIII protocols), distributing critical functions across available members until a permanent replacement is assigned through role-assignment

## J. Expiry / Review Condition

Departure Records do not expire -- they are permanent governance records. The voluntary-exit process itself is reviewed annually through the ACT consent process to ensure the timeline defaults, coordinator assignment, and communication protocols remain appropriate. If any ecosystem member identifies a pattern of departure friction (e.g., consistent coordinator delays, social pressure reports), they may request a governance health audit (Layer VII) focused on exit process integrity. Re-entry eligibility does not expire unless the departing member's foundational agreement contained a specific re-entry window.

## K. Exit Compatibility Check

This skill is the exit process. It is self-referentially compatible: a member who is departing uses this skill to depart. The skill ensures that no other skill in the NEOS stack can override the right to exit. If any skill's process would prevent or delay departure beyond 30 days, this skill takes precedence. The Departure Record created by this skill feeds into the portable-record skill for the member's governance history and into the re-entry-integration skill if the member later returns.

## L. Cross-Unit Interoperability Impact

When a member departs a single ETHOS but remains in the ecosystem, the departure affects only that ETHOS's roles and agreements. The member's ecosystem-level identity and agreements remain intact. When a member departs the entire ecosystem, all ETHOS are notified through the standard communication channel. Cross-ecosystem departure (leaving a federated NEOS network) follows the same process at each ecosystem level -- the member departs each ecosystem individually, receiving a portable record from each. The departure process and record format are standardized across all NEOS ecosystems to ensure interoperability when members move between ecosystems.
